STRATEGY RULES

D1 sweeps: PDL, PWL, PML, EQL and major swing lows for bullish bias; PDH, PWH, PMH, EQH and major swing highs for bearish bias. A valid sweep trades beyond the level and closes back inside it. D1 confirmation accepts the close beyond the previous candle high/low, engulfing, rejection, displacement, or the specified three-candle inside-bar pattern.

H1 confirmation searches for a matching H1 candle sweep after D1 confirmation. In MANUAL D1 BIAS mode, automatic D1 confirmation is bypassed. H1 confirmation uses completed candles whose close time is after you apply the manual bias; a candle already closed before then is excluded. Fibonacci is drawn across the full high-low range of the H1 sweep candle.

M5 first entry: touch of 0.5; SL at 0.618 and TP at 0. BUY stop must be below entry; SELL stop must be above entry. Second entry: wait for a completed M5 candle to sweep 0.618 and close back across it; enter at that candle close, with BUY SL at its low or SELL SL at its high; TP remains Fib 0. The 4-pip ($0.40 quoted-price) stop-distance cap applies only to Gold second entries.
