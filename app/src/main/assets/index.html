STRATEGY RULES

D1 sweeps: PDL, PWL, PML, EQL and major swing lows for bullish bias; PDH, PWH, PMH, EQH and major swing highs for bearish bias. A valid sweep trades beyond the level and closes back inside it. D1 confirmation accepts the close beyond the previous candle high/low, engulfing, rejection, displacement, or the specified three-candle inside-bar pattern.

H1 confirmation searches earlier H1 candle highs/lows for a matching sweep after D1 confirmation. The current implementation searches up to 500 earlier H1 candles. Fibonacci is drawn across the full high-low range of the H1 sweep candle.

M5 first entry: touch of 0.5; requested SL 0.618 and TP 1. Second entry: sweep of 0.618 and close back across; requested BUY stop is the sweep candle high and SELL stop is its low; TP 1.

Risk validation: the specified stop-loss levels are on the wrong side of the entry under the specified Fibonacci direction. The app preserves your requested levels but blocks those setups instead of presenting them as safe or executable trades.
